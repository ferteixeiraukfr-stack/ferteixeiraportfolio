# Driving Support Efficiency & Reducing Incident Volume

## Context

High-growth cybersecurity enterprise experiencing recurring support incidents and delayed MTTR due to product complexity (Cloud DLP, CASB, policy engines).

## Challenge

- Repetitive incident volume
- Escalations from L1/L2 to Tier 3
- SLA pressure on service-critical incidents
- Low documentation-driven self-service adoption

## Actions

- Owned Tier 3 escalations end-to-end
- Analysed recurring ticket patterns
- Built structured troubleshooting playbooks
- Standardised resolution workflows
- Collaborated with engineering on systemic fixes

## Impact

- Reduced repeat escalations in 40%
- Improved MTTR to 30 days
- Increased support team capability
- Enhanced customer confidence
